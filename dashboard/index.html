The vantage finding

Same portals, two vantage points

A portal measured from inside its country and from the nearest foreign region gives two different answers. The gap is the cost of distance, not a difference in the portals — and it is the result a single-vantage study cannot produce.

    The five-pillar standard

    10 sub-features · 20 columns

    An earlier iteration carried more than seventy uncalibrated columns. Most were correlated restatements of the same measurement, which makes a composite index arbitrary. The standard now holds exactly five orthogonal pillars and two defensible sub-features each, every one traceable to a published specification.

    Composite index

    Weighted, 0–100

    The composite is a fixed linear weighting, not a fitted model. Accessibility carries the largest weight because it is the only pillar with a statutory obligation behind it under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act. Security follows because header absence is a measurable defect rather than a judgement call.

    Speed is scored as max(0, 100 − 15 × LCP), so a portal at the 2.5 s Core Web Vitals threshold scores 62.5 on that term and a portal at 6 s scores zero. The two binary reach terms are deliberately small: they describe coverage, not quality.

    Composite indexweights

M = 0.30 * accessibility

    What the data cannot tell you

    • 01

      Automated testing is not conformance

      axe-core catches a subset of WCAG failures. It cannot judge cognitive load, screen-reader audio flow or reading order. A domain scoring well here is free of the machine-detectable defects we tested for; it is not thereby WCAG-conformant.

    • 02

      LCP is estimated, never measured

      Largest Contentful Paint is derived from time to first byte and payload size. A true LCP needs a browser paint trace, which we did not run at scale.

    • 03

      Nearest probes are not domestic

      180 countries were measured from a probe in another country. The domestic_probe column says which. Filter on it before making a claim about what a citizen experiences.

    • 04

      Discovery is not verification

      A domain in the registry was observed in an auditable source. It was not confirmed to be an operational citizen portal, which is why reachability is 30% rather than near 100%.

    • 05

      One snapshot

      This release is a single observation. The dataset carries no per-domain timestamp series, so no trend claim is supported.

    CivicWeb Observatory

    A cross-country observatory of public-sector web infrastructure, maintained by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Kharagpur. Each measurement records the vantage point that produced it, so a domestic reading is never confused with a foreign one.
